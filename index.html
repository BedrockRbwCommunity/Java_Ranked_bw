About Ranked Bedwars

What is Ranked Bedwars?

Ranked Bedwars is a game mode on our Minecraft server. We play 4v4 Bedwars games with team calls in Discord and a competitive matchmaking and ranking system. It’s the best way to learn Bedwars and become a better player, while also winning cash prizes and status.

How to Play

From queue to victory

Five steps from joining the server to ranking up.

1

Link your Minecraft and Discord accounts

Join the server and run /link to get a code, then use /link code with our Discord bot. You only need to do this once.

2

Queue a game

Join the call labelled Queue [0-600] and wait for it to fill to 8 players. You must be online on the Minecraft server. Once you rank up, you’ll join Queue [600+].

3

Pick your team

Two team captains are selected and take turns picking their teammates. The bot then moves you into your team call.

4

Vote for a map

Five Bedwars maps are offered to everyone in the queue. The map with the most votes gets played.

5

Play and rank up

Work with your team to win. At the end you gain ELO, climb the ranks and get a full stat sheet for the game.
